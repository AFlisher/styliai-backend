Deleting your StyliAI account

This page exists to satisfy Google Play's requirement that an app offering account creation publishes a publicly reachable URL explaining how to request account and data deletion, in addition to the in-app path. It is deliberately written so that it can be read and acted on by someone who does not have the app installed.

How to delete your account

From inside the app (immediate)

  1. Open StyliAI and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll to Delete Account.
  4. Read the confirmation screen, type DELETE to confirm, and — if you sign in with a password — enter your current password.
  5. Confirm. Your account is erased immediately and you are signed out of every device.

There is no waiting period, no grace period and no recovery window. Deletion cannot be undone.

If you cannot access the app

If you have lost access to your account or removed the app, email [[PLACEHOLDER: support email]] from the address registered to the account and ask for deletion. We will verify that you control the address before acting.

What is deleted

All of the following are erased when you delete your account:

CategoryIncludes
Account and identityEmail address, display name, password hash, Google sign-in link, country
ProfileBiography, profile picture (both the record and the stored image file)
Your imagesEvery generated image and thumbnail in your gallery, and the stored image files themselves
CreditsBalance, transaction history, daily reward history, ad reward records
ActivityFavourites, notifications, generation history, feedback and ratings
SessionsAll refresh tokens and device records; every signed-in device is signed out at once
Abuse recordsRisk score and any findings associated with the account

What is kept, and why

Two things survive deletion. Both are deliberate, and neither contains information that identifies you.

KeptWhat it containsWhy
A deletion record The account's internal identifier (a random UUID), the date and time, and counts of what was removed. No email, name, IP address, photo or content. So we can demonstrate that the deletion was actually carried out if you, a regulator, or an app store asks. Once the account is gone, nothing else remembers it existed.
Anti-fraud ledger entries A short-lived record that a device-integrity token was used. The link to your account is removed at deletion; the remaining entry expires on its own, by default within 24 hours. Deleting these immediately would let a token that was already spent be replayed against the credit system.

Administrative records of actions taken by our staff (for example, a suspension) are retained as an accountability trail. These record what was done and by whom, not your personal data.

Limits we cannot control

Please read this before deleting. When you generate an image, your photo is sent to a third-party AI provider that performs the transformation. Depending on the provider in use, your photo may have been copied to that provider's own content delivery network, with a retention period we do not control and no deletion mechanism available to us.

Deleting your account removes every copy we hold. It cannot retract copies already transferred to that provider, which expire according to the provider's own retention policy. We strip embedded metadata — including any GPS coordinates — from every photo before it leaves our systems, so what is transferred does not carry your camera or location details.
