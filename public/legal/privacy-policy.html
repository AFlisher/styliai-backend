1. Who this policy covers

This policy explains what StyliAI (“the app”) collects, why, who it is shared with, and what you can do about it. It applies to the StyliAI mobile app and the backend service that supports it.

3. What we do not collect

  • We do not collect precise location. Country is inferred locally from IP and nothing finer is stored.
  • We do not collect your contacts, calendar, microphone, or SMS.
  • We do not perform biometric identification. Photos are processed to apply a visual style, not to identify a person.
  • We do not sell personal data.

4. Your photos, and where they go

This is the most important section of this policy, so it is stated plainly.

  • When you generate an image, the photo you selected is uploaded to our backend and then forwarded to a third-party AI image-generation provider that performs the actual transformation. We do not run these models ourselves.
  • Before your photo leaves our infrastructure, embedded metadata (EXIF/XMP/IPTC) is stripped server-side. This removes camera information and any GPS coordinates your photo may have carried.
  • Depending on the provider in use, your source photo may be uploaded to that provider's own content delivery network under a URL we do not control, with a retention period we do not control, and with no deletion call available to us. This is currently the case for the provider in production. Where that applies, deleting your account removes the copies we hold but cannot retract copies already transferred to that provider. See the Account Deletion Policy.
  • Prompts and images may be processed by the provider's own safety classifiers. A generation may be refused on those grounds.

9. Deleting your account

You can delete your account from inside the app at any time: Profile → Delete Account. Deletion is immediate and irreversible: your profile, photos you uploaded, generated images, credit balance and history, favourites, notifications and sessions are erased, and every signed-in device is signed out at once.

We retain one record that a deletion took place. It contains no email, name, IP address or content — only the deleted account's internal identifier, a timestamp and counts — and exists so we can demonstrate that the erasure was carried out. Full detail, including the limits described in §4, is in the Account Deletion Policy.

10. Security

Traffic between the app and our backend is encrypted in transit and the app pins the certificates it will accept. Passwords are stored only as bcrypt hashes. Your generated images and profile picture are held in private storage and served over short-lived signed links rather than public URLs. No system is perfectly secure, and we do not claim otherwise.
